Can I use my Ticket Auto balance at Ticket Restaurant outlets?

Your Ticket Auto balance can be used exclusively on the network of stations allocated to your card cannot be used elsewhere under any condition.

My favorite nearby station is not listed, how can I list it?

We constantly look to grow our network of stations, please email us your request on info-lb@edenred.com your request and if the station meets our requirements, we will be in touch with its management immediately. Please note that we don’t guarantee that all requested stations will join our network.

Do I always have to carry my Ticket Auto card with me?

If you have already added your Ticket Auto card to your account on this app, then you don’t always need to carry the card with you.

Can another user use my Ticket Auto account?

You can share your card Ticket Auto card details with another user who can add it to his/her profile and use it simultaneously.
