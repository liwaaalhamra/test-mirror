Can I use my Ticket Restaurant balance for Ticket Auto products?

Your Ticket Restaurant balance can be used exclusively on the Ticket Restaurant network and cannot be used elsewhere under any condition.

My favorite outlet is not listed, how can I list it?

We constantly look to grow our network, please email us your request on info-lb@edenred.com and we will be in touch with the provided outlet(s) immediately. Please note that we don’t guarantee that all provided outlets will join our network.

Do I always have to carry my Ticket Restaurant card with me?

If you have already added your Ticket Restaurant card to your account on this app, then you don’t always need to carry the card with you.

Can another user use my Ticket Restaurant account?

You can share your Ticket Restaurant card details with another user who can add it to his/her profile and use it simultaneously.
